# Product FAQ

## Pricing

### How much does it cost?

Our pricing starts at $29/month for the Basic plan, $79/month for Professional, 
and $199/month for Enterprise. All plans include a 14-day free trial.

### Do you offer refunds?

Yes, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied with our 
product, contact support for a full refund.

### Are there any setup fees?

No, there are no setup fees for any of our plans.

## Features

### What integrations do you support?

We integrate with Slack, Microsoft Teams, Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, and 
over 100 other popular business tools via our API and Zapier.

### Is there an API?

Yes, all paid plans include API access. The Basic plan includes 1,000 API calls 
per month, Professional includes 10,000, and Enterprise includes unlimited calls.

### Can I export my data?

Yes, you can export all your data at any time in CSV or JSON format from the 
Settings > Export page.

## Support

### How do I contact support?

Email us at support@example.com or use the chat widget in the app. 
Enterprise customers have access to phone support.

### What are your support hours?

Standard support is available Monday-Friday, 9am-6pm EST.
Enterprise customers have access to 24/7 support.
